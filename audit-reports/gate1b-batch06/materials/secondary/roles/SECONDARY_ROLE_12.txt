Industrial Engineering Director – India GETAHEAD India | Full-time leadership opportunity GETAHEAD is an international sourcing, supplier development and industrialization company supporting European and international customers in developing capable manufacturing partners across Asia. We are expanding our activities in India and are looking for an experienced Industrial Engineering Director who can take ownership of supplier qualification, industrialization, ramp-up and continuous improvement projects across the country. This is not a purchasing role. We are looking for an experienced manufacturing professional who can walk into a supplier's plant, understand its processes and organization, identify weaknesses and risks, and lead the supplier toward the required technical, quality and production level. A key part of the role is working at the interface between the supplier and the different technical specialists required to industrialize and stabilize production. Depending on the project, these may include testing laboratories, tooling and fixture experts, manufacturing/process engineers, quality specialists, metrology experts and customer engineering teams. The Director does not need to be the deepest technical specialist in every discipline. However, he or she must understand how these disciplines interact, know when specialist expertise is required, and be capable of coordinating the different parties toward a stable production process. What Makes This Role Different GETAHEAD does not intend to build a traditional sourcing office in India. Our objective is to build an industrial capability on the ground that can independently assess suppliers and then stay involved until identified technical gaps have been resolved and reliable production has been established. That means our work does not end with a supplier audit or an assessment report. Where required, we coordinate the laboratories, tooling specialists, engineers and other technical resources necessary to help suppliers progress from initial qualification through ramp-up to stable production. The person joining us in this position will therefore have substantial influence over how this capability is built in India. For the right candidate, the position offers a path toward broader leadership responsibility as our India activities grow. We are also developing a long-term equity-based bonus participation program for senior leadership, with eligibility beginning after one year with the company. Our intention is to reward executives who help build lasting enterprise value rather than optimize only for short-term targets. Selection Process Because our work is highly practical, we do not want to make a senior hiring decision based only on interviews. After an initial interview process, selected finalist candidates may be invited to accompany GETAHEAD during an actual supplier assessment in India. This will be structured as a paid professional assessment/project engagement, with agreed compensation for time spent and reimbursement of approved travel expenses. This gives both sides the opportunity to experience working together in a real manufacturing environment before making a long-term commitment.
