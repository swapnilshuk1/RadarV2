# RADAR v2 — Editorial Principles & Authoring Style Guide (Version 2.1 · August 2026)

This document governs all narrative copy, editorial patterns, and text composition within RADAR v2. All patterns added to the Editorial Registry must comply strictly with these principles.

---

## 1. Core Editorial Invariants

### 1.1 Retained Invariants

I. **Evidence-Grounded Truth**: Never assert evidence, reporting lines, P&L scale, or founder proximity that the evidence graph or job projection does not explicitly support. An unverifiable claim is not a strong claim; it is a liability the reader will discover in the first call.

II. **Certainty Matched to Confidence**: Editorial confidence (`assertive` | `balanced` | `cautious`) tracks assessment confidence exactly. Superlatives require benchmark data. “One of the strongest” is permitted only where the comparison set is defined.

III. **Advice Over Description**: Frame every insight as a trusted partner would. “Proceed this week; validate the reporting line on the first call” — not “This position is based in Gurugram.”

IV. **Document-Level Coherence**: Every pattern tells one story across headline, opening, bridge, boundaries, and closing. If the closing could be swapped into another pattern without friction, the pattern has no thesis.

V. **Frozen Navigation Landmarks**: Structural section headers (`EXECUTIVE BRIEF`, `STRATEGIC CAREER VALUE`, `THE CASE`, `THE ROLE`, `YOUR ADVANTAGE`, `OPEN QUESTIONS`, `DECISION BOUNDARIES`, `SUPPORTING EVIDENCE`, `DOSSIER LEDGER`) are permanent UI landmarks and must **never** be altered.

### 1.2 Additional Invariants (Version 2.1)

VI. **Name the Consequence**: Every brief must state what changes if the reader acts, and what is forfeited if they do not. A brief without a consequence is a summary.

VII. **One Concrete Anchor Per Brief**: At least one number, named function, named counterparty, or dated event must appear in the composed narrative. Abstractions may support a specific; they may not substitute for one.

VIII. **Risk in the First Clause**: Where a pattern has a material risk, the pause-if line names it before it qualifies it. Never bury the reservation behind a subordinate clause.

IX. **No Two Patterns Share a Skeleton**: Sentence architecture is part of the voice. Two patterns may share a subject; they may not share a grammatical shape in the same slot. This is enforced at review and pattern selection, not just runtime.

---

## 2. Voice and Cadence

- **Register**: Financial Times leader column; Spencer Stuart position specification; Economist briefing. Measured, authoritative, unhurried. The reader is a peer, not an audience.
- **Sentence Length**: 28 words is the ceiling; 18 is the target. Alternate lengths deliberately — a long analytical sentence followed by a short verdict is the core rhythm of the form.
- **Punctuation**: Headlines end with a full stop. Semicolons are permitted and encouraged for balanced clauses. Em dashes sparingly, and never twice in a paragraph.
- **Prose Cleanliness**: No ampersands (`&`) in running text (use "and"). No internal system telemetry surfaced to the reader.

## Annexure A — Path to Better Execution

1. **Enrich the variable set**: Include funding stage, last raise date, headcount band, reporting line, team size, revenue band, and named competitor set.
2. **Make closing slot conviction-aware**: Closings inherit shortlist tier so dossier final line and shortlist grouping never contradict each other.
3. **Add skeleton tag per pattern**: One-line annotation (`fact-first`, `comparison-first`, `consequence-first`, `observation-first`).
4. **Instrument slot-level analytics**: Measure headline and closing performance independently.
5. **Session repetition metric**: Enforce pattern skeleton and family diversity across session selections.

## Annexure B — Frozen Landmarks and Change Control

The following section headers are permanent UI landmarks:

`EXECUTIVE BRIEF` · `STRATEGIC CAREER VALUE` · `THE CASE` · `THE ROLE` · `YOUR ADVANTAGE` · `OPEN QUESTIONS` · `DECISION BOUNDARIES` · `SUPPORTING EVIDENCE` · `DOSSIER LEDGER`

They must not be renamed, reordered, translated, abbreviated, or conditionally hidden.
