# RADAR Editorial Principles & Authoring Style Guide (v1.0.0)

This document governs all narrative copy, editorial patterns, and text composition within RADAR v2. All patterns added to the Editorial Registry must comply strictly with these principles.

---

## 1. Core Editorial Invariants

1. **Evidence-Grounded Truth**: Never claim evidence, reporting lines, P&L scale, or founder proximity that is not explicitly verified by the evidence graph or job projections.
2. **Never Exaggerate Certainty**: Match editorial confidence (`assertive` | `balanced` | `cautious`) strictly to assessment confidence. Avoid superlatives unless backed by comparative benchmark data.
3. **Prefer Executive Advice Over Descriptive Summary**: Frame role insights from the perspective of an executive advisor or partner (e.g. *"Proceed this week; validate reporting line during initial call"* rather than *"This job is located in Gurugram"*).
4. **Coherent Document-Level Narrative**: Every Editorial Pattern must tell a single, unified story (e.g. Commercial Ownership Expansion) across headline, opening summary, bridge, and closing.
5. **Frozen Navigation Landmarks**: Structural section headers (`EXECUTIVE BRIEF`, `STRATEGIC CAREER VALUE`, `THE CASE`, `THE ROLE`, `YOUR ADVANTAGE`, `OPEN QUESTIONS`, `DECISION BOUNDARIES`, `SUPPORTING EVIDENCE`, `DOSSIER LEDGER`) are permanent UI landmarks and must **never** be altered.

---

## 2. Voice & Register
- **Register**: Financial Times / Spencer Stuart / Economist executive briefing style.
- **Tone**: Measured, authoritative, crisp, and objective.
- **Punctuation & Grammar**: Headlines end with full stops. Sentences are grammatically complete and avoids marketing buzzwords.
