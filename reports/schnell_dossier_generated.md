# Executive Dossier: Business Head - Sales & Transaction Advisory — Schnell Builders Pvt. Ltd.

**Verdict:** PASS

### Rationale
Swapnil Shukla is a highly accomplished marketing and digital transformation leader with extensive experience in building and scaling teams, managing P&L for large portfolios, and driving digital revenue growth. However, the core requirements of this Business Head role are deeply rooted in real estate sales, high-value transaction closure, and originating third-party sales mandates. The candidate lacks direct, demonstrable experience in these specific domains, which are mandatory for this greenfield sales leadership position. While transferable skills in team building, P&L management, and digital lead generation exist, they do not sufficiently cover the mandatory real estate sales and transaction advisory expertise required to build this new business line from scratch.
